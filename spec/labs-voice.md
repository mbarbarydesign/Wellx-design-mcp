# Wellx Labs — Voice & tone

"Say the number. Say where it came from. Stop." Wellx writes like a competent colleague
sending a short message: what happened, what it means, what to do. No warmth performed, no
urgency manufactured, no apology for the software.

## Five principles
1. **Lead with the fact.** "Three claims share a repair shop." — not "We noticed something interesting."
2. **Name the actor.** "The model flagged this" or "Sara approved this." Never a passive "this was flagged."
3. **Give the confidence.** If the machine is guessing, say how much — a number, not a hedge.
4. **One instruction.** An empty state, an error and a notification each contain exactly one next action.
5. **No exclamation marks.** Anywhere. Including in celebration. (No emoji in product copy, documents, email or advertising.)

## In practice
- EMPTY STATE — yes: "No claims in this queue. / Assign one from Unallocated." · no: "You're all caught up! 🎉 Nothing to see here."
- ERROR — yes: "Bind failed. The policy start date is in the past. / Change the date and retry." · no: "Oops! Something went wrong. Please try again later."
- AI OUTPUT — yes: "Flagged for review · confidence 0.74 / Three claims on this policy share a repair shop." · no: "Our AI thinks this might be worth a look!"
- CONFIRMATION — yes: "Policy POL/2026/88431 bound. / Documents sent to the broker." · no: "Success! You did it. Great job!"

## Tone by audience
- **Member** — second person, present tense, no jargon. Never "claim adjudication"; always "we are reviewing your claim." No ratios; no policy language unless quoting the policy.
- **Corporate** — population, not individuals; cost and outcome. Never name an employee in a report a manager can read.
- **Insurer** — terse and technical; domain vocabulary is expected and correct (attritional, IBNR, treaty year, combined ratio).
- **Broker** — position and movement: what changed in the book, what needs an action today.
- **Regulator** — complete sentences, full provenance, no marketing adjectives anywhere.

## Banned words
seamless · effortless · revolutionary · world-class · leverage · synergy · journey · unlock ·
empower · delight · simply · just · easy · robust · best-in-class · game-changing · disrupt · holistic.
Advertising additionally denies: "world-leading", timers, "last chance", any scarcity framing
(regulated category), and stock photography.
