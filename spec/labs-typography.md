# Wellx Labs — Typography, scale, grid & spacing

Two faces, one for speaking and one for measuring (WX-T-01…11). Never mix with the portal
system's Manrope/Alexandria.

## Figtree — speaks
Display, headings, UI copy. **Four weights only: 400, 600, 700, 800** — no 300, 500 or 900,
and **never italic**. Tracking −0.02em above 24px, −0.03em above 60px, 0 below 24px. Body
line height 1.6; display 0.95–1.15. Never used to typeset "wellx".

## IBM Plex Mono — measures
If a human would read it aloud as a figure, a code or a state, it is mono: identifiers
(POL/2026/88431), money (AED 4,182,900.00), ratios (61.4%), confidence (0.91), timestamps
(2026-08-24 14:07:22 GST), caps labels (UNDERWRITING · PENDING REVIEW), the LABS chip, chart
axes. **Weights 400 and 500 only.** Caps labels track +0.14 to +0.22em; lowercase mono never
tracks. Never running body copy or headings.

## The scale — eight steps (size/weight/tracking)
Display XL 96/800/−0.045em · Display 64/700/−0.038 · Title 44/700/−0.03 · Heading 30/700/−0.024
· Subhead 22/600/−0.018 · Lead 19/400/−0.008 · Body 16/400/0 · Small 14/400/0.
"A design that needs a ninth step is solving a hierarchy problem with type size instead of space."

## In print & on slides
Print body min 12pt/16px, captions 10.5pt/14px, mono labels 8.5pt/11px tracked uppercase;
measure 62–78 characters. Slides: 24px absolute minimum at 1920×1080, titles 60–96px.
Mobile: 16px body min, 44px hit targets.

## Denials
WX-T-08: no third typeface — two faces, both free and hosted; no display face, no serif, no
icon font. WX-T-09: no justified text — ragged right always, no hyphenation at display sizes.

## Grid, baseline & spacing (WX-T-10/11)
- **One grid everywhere**: 12 columns · 12px gutter · margins 34/44. Content spans whole columns.
- **8px baseline** — every vertical measurement is a multiple; no arbitrary margins.
- Space scale: **4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128**.
- Radius scale: **4 · 6 · 9 · 14 · 999. Nothing between.**
- Elevation: three shadows on light; borders only on dark.
- **Three densities** (chosen from session length, not taste — a surface picks one and holds it):
  Editorial 32px rhythm · 56px rows · 19px lead (member/marketing) · Balanced 24 · 44 · 16
  (corporate/broker) · Operating 16 · 36 · 14 (insurer console). Rows never below 32px.
