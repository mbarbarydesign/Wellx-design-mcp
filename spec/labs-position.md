# Wellx Labs — Position: soul, constitution, laws & governance

From the System Manual, Release 01 · 2026 — "Governing. Supersedes all prior guidance."
Every rule carries an identifier (WX-L constitution · WX-P principles · WX-M mark · WX-C colour
· WX-T typography · WX-S sound · WX-N motion/icons/material · WX-I interface · WX-A
applications; suffix ·DENY marks a prohibition). An identifier never changes meaning; withdrawn
rules are retired, not reused.

## The Brand Soul (WX-L-00 — canonical)
**"Insurance becomes invisible. Humans become alive."** Insurance is the business of risk —
complex because risk is complex, regulated because consequences matter. Labs exists to help
understand complexity: better · earlier · with greater context · through the lens of
possibility. For people managing risk: intelligence creates greater understanding and
foresight. For people living with risk: protection becomes proactive, adaptive and valuable
without demanding more attention from life. "Risk management moves behind the scenes. Life
comes forward." The Brand Soul is canonical — never rewritten to suit a surface.

## The Constitution — eight canonical laws (WX-L-01…08)
1. **Value before technology.** Technology is the means; human value is the measure.
2. **Build with. Never for.** Build what neither could create alone.
3. **Know deeply. Question constantly.** Curiosity without expertise is uncertainty; expertise without curiosity becomes constraint.
4. **Mould to possibility.** Build where possibility creates outcomes that matter.
5. **Carry complexity. Reveal possibility.** The purpose of understanding risk is not simply to take less of it — it is to know where we can take more.
6. **Bridge, don't disrupt.** Scale, trust, regulatory experience, underwriting knowledge, capital and institutional memory are not baggage; established insurers and innovators create together.
7. **Intelligence, not AI.** Never ask "Where can we insert AI?" — ask "What needs to be understood to make this possibility real?" AI, actuarial models, data, human judgment, institutional experience and context are all sources of intelligence.
8. **Multiply value.** Value moves outward and compounds. Do not merely capture value — multiply it.
Meta-rules: WX-L-09 a constitutional law is never overridden by a design rule, token, component
or deadline · WX-L-10 where a specification and a law disagree, the law governs and the
specification is withdrawn · WX-L-11 new work is tested against the eight laws before it is
tested against the system.

## The Value Loop (WX-L-12/13)
Doctrine, drawn as a closed loop in this order with equal weight on every stage:
**RISK → INTELLIGENCE → INSIGHT → POSSIBILITY → VALUE → LEARNING → (back to risk).**
Learning feeds back into a richer understanding of risk. ·DENY: never drawn as a growth curve,
a funnel, a product diagram or a consultancy flywheel.

## Six-layer authority
00 Brand Soul (canonical) · 01 Constitution (canonical) · 02 Design principles (canonical) ·
03 Design grammar — colour, type, signal, motion, data, density, voice, sound, composition
(specification) · 04 Implementation rules — tokens, components, denials (specification) ·
05 Governance (canonical). Each layer governs the one below. "If a page appears to contradict
the Soul or the Constitution, the page is wrong." 

## The thesis
Build for the operator; let everyone else overhear it. The hardest surface — an insurer
console forty thousand rows deep — sets the rules; every softer surface inherits them and
relaxes. Calm by default. Coloured only where the machine has something to say.

## The eight laws
A law is not a preference. If a design breaks one, the design is wrong.
1. **Calm is the default state.** Nothing moves, glows or saturates unless it carries information the operator needs now.
2. **One signal per view.** A single element may claim priority. If two elements glow, the operator trusts neither.
3. **Colour is evidence, not decoration.** Saturated colour means the system has computed something. Chrome, layout and body copy stay neutral, always.
4. **The mark is fixed.** The geometry, proportion and width modulation of the bionic mark and wordmark never change.
5. **Density is a courtesy.** Operators read faster than they scroll. Compress rows, never words.
6. **Provenance travels with the number.** Any figure the machine produced shows where it came from and how sure it is.
7. **Silence is a design decision.** Absence of sound, motion or colour is specified as deliberately as their presence.
8. **The system expands by registration.** New products, segments and surfaces take a slot in the existing system. They do not earn new hues, faces or marks.

## The seven principles
- WX-P-01 **Legibility before beauty.** If a choice reduces reading speed for an operator, it loses.
- WX-P-02 **Hierarchy by weight, not by hue.** Size, weight and space carry structure; colour carries meaning.
- WX-P-03 **Borders on dark, shadows on light.** Separation technique follows the field, never taste.
- WX-P-04 **Mono measures.** Anything countable, timestamped or identified is set in IBM Plex Mono.
- WX-P-05 **Motion explains causality.** An element moves only to show where it came from.
- WX-P-06 **Round numbers are suspicious.** Display real precision; never pad a figure to look tidy.
- WX-P-07 **Every surface names itself.** A screenshot taken out of context still says which product it is.
- WX-P-08 **Authority in tone, never dogma.** The book states what is decided and names what is still being learned.
Precedence: a principle is binding until it meets a law — the law wins and the principle is
amended next release. Where two principles disagree, the lower number wins. Citation etiquette:
"This fails WX-P-02" is a review; "this feels busy" is not.

## What we are not (five declined positions)
1. **Not a wellness brand** — no soft gradients, no lifestyle warmth, no rounded optimism.
2. **Not a consumer fintech** — no neobank grammar: cards, confetti, illustrated mascots.
3. **Not an enterprise incumbent** — no navy, no serif, no columned facade, no handshake.
4. **Not an AI novelty** — the spectrum is not a sparkle; intelligence shows through provenance and confidence, not iconography.
5. **Not a design showcase** — this is a constraint document; nothing exists to demonstrate craft.

## Governance
1. **The book governs.** Where it conflicts with a file, a Figma library, a slide or a memory of a meeting, the book wins.
2. **Design owns the system.** Rule changes are made by Design and released as numbered versions. No local overrides.
3. **Products override only where named:** Command Center owns the purple action accent (#6A2BD8 light / #9B6DFF dark) and the ambient bed; Care Connect owns rose. Nothing else is overridden.
4. **New rules take the next identifier.** If it is not in the register, it is not a rule.
5. **Review is against denials.** The fastest review is to read the denial rules and look for violations; everything else is judgement.
Release 01 · 2026; reviewed twice yearly; amendments as 01.1, 01.2. Release 02 arrives when a
fifth segment or a second typeface case arises.

## Levels of authority & the register
- **Constitution** (Part 00 + the eight laws): changes extremely rarely, by the founders — never amended to accommodate a design decision.
- **Principles · laws**: evolve through major releases, owned by Design.
- **Specifications** (tokens, components, geometry, grids, denials): evolve through minor releases (01.1, 01.2).
- **Proposed**: specified during design but not yet validated in production — does not govern until promoted by Design. **At Release 01, Part 05 (Sound) is proposed; everything else is canonical or specification.**
Register record per rule: id WX-{L|P|M|C|T|S|N|I|A}-{nn} · level · status
(canonical|specification|proposed) · owner (founders|design|product) · version · scope ·
rationale · inherits · validation (none|production|measured). Retired at Release 01: none.
