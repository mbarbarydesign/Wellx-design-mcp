# Shape, spacing, widths

## Radius
Scale: none 0 · xxs 2 · xs 4 · sm 6 · md 8 · lg 10 · xl 12 · 2xl 16 · 3xl 20 · 4xl 24 · full 9999
Component mapping (fixed):
- Cards, panels, dialogs, chat bubbles: **16 (radius-2xl)**
- Buttons (all variants): **12 (radius-xl)**
- Inputs, selects: **8 (radius-md)**
- Sidebar nav pill, small tiles: 10 (radius-lg)
- Pills, search input, avatars, dots, toggles: full
- Checkboxes: 4 (radius-xs)

## Spacing (4px base)
xs 4 · sm 6 · md 8 · lg 12 · xl 16 (gutter) · 2xl 20 · 3xl 24 (section) · 4xl 32 · 5xl 40 · 6xl 48 · 7xl 64 …
- Page padding: 24 (lg screens 32)
- Section rhythm & card inner padding: 20/24
- Table rows ~76px default (~52px compact); controls 40 default / 36 sm / 32 pills

## Widths
- Tab content max width on big screens: **1440px (width-4xl), centered** — never edge-to-edge tables/dashboards
- Forms & settings: 768px (width-xl); paragraphs: 720px
