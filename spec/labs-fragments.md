# Wellx Labs — The mark's construction & the fragment system

## Construction (WX-M-05)
The bionic mark is one continuous ribbon that resolves into a lowercase w — describe it as
"a continuous ribbon" (never a wave, signature, heartbeat or brushstroke).
- Cage 16 × 10 units; ribbon inscribed in 14 × 7.5. 1 unit = cage height ÷ 10.
- Ribbon width modulates 0.4 → 1.9 units (thinnest at the left taper, widest at the second crest).
- Two crossings at 4.6 and 9.2 units — always visible overlaps, never merged.
- Terminal loop spans 4.1 units, closes to a 0.5-unit aperture — never fully closed.
- Align to the ribbon axis (horizontal centreline of the cage), not the bounding box.
- These figures describe the mark, they are not a licence to rebuild it: **use the released files**.

## Minimum sizes & reduction (WX-M-08/09/10)
Full lockup 180px / 45mm · mark + chip 96px / 24mm · mark alone 28px / 7mm · loop only 16px / 4mm.
Reduce by dropping components (chip → divider → wordmark → ribbon, keeping the terminal loop),
never by shrinking below minimums. Never scale non-uniformly. Clear space = 1× chip height on
all sides, measured from the cage; glow and shadow from neighbours count as intrusion (WX-M-06/07).

## The fragment system — "Fragments organise. Only the lockup signs."
The ribbon decomposes into exactly three licensed fragments (WX-M-25):
- **The crest** — a rule that swells and thins: dividers, underlines, wayfinding routes.
- **The loop** — a container: frames, encircles, tracks a progress. Keeps its open aperture — never closed into a circle (WX-M-27).
- **The taper** — a point: marks, bullets, locates. The only fragment that may carry a spectrum hue (WX-M-28).
A fragment is furniture, never a signature (WX-M-26).

## Seven sanctioned uses
1. **Watermark** — loop at 4–6% ink on light / 5–8% paper on dark, bleeding off exactly two adjacent edges, min height 1.2× page height, one per page (WX-M-29/30/31).
2. **Pattern tiles** — four tiles on a 16-unit repeat: ribbon repeat (endpapers, packaging), loop field (decks, backdrops), signal grid (data surfaces), taper field (motion/sound applications). Low-saturation spectrum allowed; never the whole mark (WX-M-32).
3. **App icon & favicon** — tile #050509, corner radius 22% of width, mark at 70% of tile on the ribbon axis. Sizes 1024 / 180 / 64 / 32; at or below 16px the loop alone at 42% with 2px stroke (WX-M-34/35/36).
4. **System avatars** — for non-human actors only (service accounts, automation, unassigned). Loop at 30% paper 3px stroke + a taper whose position and hue derive from a hash of the entity id — never chosen by hand. Ten positions × five hues = fifty stable identities. Humans use photos or initials (WX-M-37/38/39).
5. **Page furniture** — section rule (16px taper + 3px modulating hairline at 55% ink); the spectrum identity rule once per document at the signature (WX-M-41); step indicators (11px tapers, current 18px); progress = loop as track, aperture as head, 1.1s/revolution linear; list bullets are 10px tapers — never a dash, chevron, tick or emoji (WX-M-40…45).
6. **Environmental** — above 2m fragments may bleed and crop; materials: brushed aluminium, ink-black acrylic, routed reveal — no plastic, chrome or backlit colour; illumination indirect; one taper in warm light may be the only coloured element in a space; crest as 12mm wayfinding route line; the lockup appears once per space, at eye height at reception (WX-M-46…51).
7. **Animated states** — three states: IDLE mark at 40%, genuinely still; THINKING the loop alone rotating 1.1s linear infinite in violet (the only place violet moves); RESOLVED full ribbon fades in 240ms cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0,1). Rotation and opacity are the only animatable properties — never scale, skew, morph, draw-on or redraw. One animated fragment per view (inherits the one-signal law). prefers-reduced-motion: final frame, thinking becomes a static 40% loop (WX-M-52…57).

## Denials (WX-M-58…65)
- No fragment as signature — a loop in a deck corner is not a logo.
- No new fragments — no arcs, crescents, half-ribbons, isolated crossings, cropped marks.
- No recomposition into a new mark, monogram, ligature or emblem.
- The loop never encircles a letter, number or initial — not a badge frame.
- Crest and loop stay monochrome (single exception: a progress track takes one accent — not the gradient).
- A loop is never a chart, gauge, donut or KPI dial.
- Tapers repeat only to count something — never as scatter or sparkle.
- No surface is named after a fragment — products take descriptors, not symbols.

## Lockup misuse (ten rejections — WX-M-15…24)
No spectrum inside the wordmark · no even-weight ribbon · no chip without the dot or stacked ·
no spectrum symbol in a lockup · no non-uniform scaling · no effects (shadow, glow, bevel, blur,
drawn container) · no rotation or mirroring · no lockup directly on photography (use a solid
plate) · no retypeset wordmark · no second accent on a product lockup.
